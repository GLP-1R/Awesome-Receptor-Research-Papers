# Contribution Guidelines

Thanks for helping keep this list complete and accurate!

## Scope

Only papers published in the MDPI journal [*Receptors*](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/receptors) (ISSN 2813-2564) are accepted. Acknowledgments to reviewers, corrections/errata, retraction notices and publisher's notes are not listed.

## How to suggest a paper

- **Open an issue** with the paper's DOI (e.g. `10.3390/receptors5040031`) and, if you like, the category you think it belongs to, **or**
- **Open a pull request** that adds the entry to `README.md` (and, ideally, the matching record to `papers.json`).

## Before you submit

1. **Check for duplicates by DOI.** Search `README.md` and `papers.json` for the DOI first.
2. **Make sure the DOI resolves** at `https://doi.org/<DOI>`.
3. **One entry per paper**, placed in exactly one main category and the best-fitting subcategory, sorted newest first within that subcategory.
4. **Use the exact entry format:**

   ```
   - [Title](https://doi.org/DOI) - Authors. *Receptors* Year, Volume(Issue), ArticleNumber.
   ```

   - Authors: list all names (Given Family) when there are three or fewer; otherwise write `FirstAuthor et al.`
   - Example: `- [Odorant Receptors and Cancer](https://doi.org/10.3390/receptors4020011) - Rafaella G. Naressi, Bettina Malnic and Luciana M. Gutiyama. *Receptors* 2025, 4(2), 11.`
5. Use the title as published, without HTML tags. No summaries or opinions.
